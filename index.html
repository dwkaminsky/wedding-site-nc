Good to know

Details, details

Is there a ceremony?+

No—this event is our wedding reception only. The celebration begins with cocktail hour at 5:00 PM.

Are children invited?+

Yes! Children are welcome at our reception. Please include them when you text your RSVP so we can plan a seat for everyone.

What should I wear?+

Aim for polished and comfortable: button-down shirts, optional ties, khakis or dress pants, sundresses, midi dresses, jumpsuits, blouses, skirts, loafers, flats, or dressy sandals. Jackets are welcome but not required. Wear something you’ll enjoy dancing in.

Will the reception be indoors?+

Yes. Cocktail hour, dinner, and dancing will all take place inside Vidrio.

Is the venue accessible?+

Please text us with any mobility or accessibility needs when you RSVP. We’ll coordinate with Vidrio so you can arrive and celebrate comfortably.
