Getting here

Fly into Asheville Regional Airport (AVL), about 25 minutes from downtown. Greenville-Spartanburg (GSP) and Charlotte (CLT) are also easy options.

✦

Getting around

Complimentary shuttles will run from all three hotels to the ceremony and back. Please don’t drink and drive.

Good to know

Details, details

Can I bring a plus-one?+

Your invitation will list everyone included in your party. If you’re unsure, enter your name on the RSVP form to see your household.

Are children invited?+

We love your little ones, but our ceremony and reception will be adults-only. We’re happy to share local sitter recommendations.

What does “garden formal” mean?+

Think suits, long or midi dresses, jumpsuits, and shoes that can handle a little grass. October evenings can be cool, so bring a layer.

Will the wedding be outdoors?+

The ceremony and cocktail hour are planned outdoors, weather permitting. Dinner and dancing will be inside a covered, climate-controlled space.

Is the venue accessible?+

Yes. The venue and shuttle are wheelchair accessible. Please note any mobility needs with your RSVP so we can make you comfortable.
